Monochrome New Tab

Every new tab is a single flat color. Pick the color of your browser toolbar, once for light mode and once for dark mode, and the new tab follows the mode by itself.

Colors

'Pick from screen' turns the pointer into an eyedropper: click the empty part of your toolbar to copy its exact color.

Vivaldi

  1. Open Settings, then Tabs, then New Tab Page.
  2. Choose Start Page and tick Controlled by Extension. Without this step Vivaldi keeps showing its Speed Dial.
  3. Leave Focus Page Content on New Tab off, so the address bar keeps the cursor.
  4. In Settings, Appearance, keep Website Appearance on Auto: the new tab then switches between light and dark together with your system.

Chrome

The new tab changes as soon as the extension is installed. If Chrome asks whether to keep the change, choose to keep it. Chrome's default toolbar is #ffffff in light mode and #3c3c3c in dark mode. Chrome may add a footer with the extension name: right-click it to hide it.

Your colors stay on this computer. Nothing is collected or sent anywhere.
