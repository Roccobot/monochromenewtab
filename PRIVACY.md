# Privacy policy

Monochrome New Tab, a browser extension by Rocco Casadei (Roccobot).
Effective date: November 25, 2026.

## The short version

Monochrome New Tab collects no data. Nothing leaves your computer.

## What the extension stores

The extension remembers two colors: the one you choose for light mode and the one you choose for
dark mode. They are saved on your computer only, in the browser's local extension storage
(`chrome.storage.local`), with a copy in the extension's own `localStorage` so that a new tab can
show the right color before it finishes loading. These values are not synced to your browser
account and are never sent anywhere.

If you never change the colors, nothing is stored at all. Clicking **Reset to defaults** in the
options deletes the stored colors, and uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data.
- It makes no network requests, and contains no analytics, ads or third-party code.
- It has no access to the websites you visit, your tabs, your history or your bookmarks.
- Its only permission is `storage`, used for the two colors described above.
